## Brand & Style

This design system powers a vibrant, peer-to-peer campus marketplace tailored for Gen-Z college students. Rejecting rigid, bureaucratic academic software, the aesthetic operates at the intersection of modern neo-brutalism, streetwear drops, and expressive social platforms like Depop and Lemon8. It feels hyper-local, spontaneous, and socially wired.

Key brand tenets:
- **Kinetic & Expressive:** High-contrast color clashes, playful micro-interactions, and punchy visual feedback evoke immediacy and campus energy.
- **Street-Smart & Credible:** Crisp outlines, chunky pill chips, and clear spatial hierarchy ensure student transactions feel secure without ever feeling sterile.
- **Hyper-Local Context:** Dedicated location-first micro-indicators (e.g., campus towers, library hubs, student dorm quads) anchor trades in real-world proximity.

## Colors

The palette leverages high-saturation focal points grounded on an ultra-light, tinted canvas.

- **Primary (`#FF334B` — Electric Vermilion):** Used for primary conversion triggers, urgent offers, high-impact CTA buttons, and brand anchors.
- **Secondary (`#7C3AED` — Cyber Violet):** Drives category tags, student seller badges, verification seals, and secondary interactive flows.
- **Tertiary (`#00E599` — Acid Mint):** Exclusively utilized for real-time affirmative cues: "En vivo", "Disponible ahora", campus proximity signals, and completed status pings. Always paired with dark text for accessibility.
- **Neutral Dark (`#12111A` — Carbon Void):** High-contrast text, structured neo-borders, and tactile drop-shadow anchors.
- **Canvas (`#FAFAFD`):** A soft, luminous pale cream-lavender tint that softens contrast compared to stark sterile white, keeping the eye fatigue low while letting vibrant pills pop.

## Typography

Typography establishes an energetic, editorial rhythm that bridges modern product design with poster-like confidence.

- **Display & Headlines (`Outfit`):** Geometric, wide-aperture display letterforms delivering confident, upbeat titling. Tightly tracked headlines reinforce a streetwise, punchy poster feel.
- **Body (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Balanced, modern grotesque with friendly, open counters ensuring effortless readability when browsing item descriptions, chat messages, and negotiation threads.
- **Labels & Micro-Tags (`Outfit`):** Heavy weight uppercase and tracking applied across sticker tags, prices, and campus zone coordinates to ensure immediate scannability.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon a flexible grid optimized for dynamic product feeds, campus drops, and mobile-first browsing:

- **Mobile (Base to 639px):** 4-column layout with 16px margins and gutters. Cards prioritize vertical stacked density with horizontal swipe reels for immediate category/tag filtering.
- **Tablet (640px to 1023px):** 8-column layout with 20px gutters and 32px margins, balancing multi-column listings alongside quick-filter drawers.
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column structure constrained to a maximum width of 1280px with 24px gutters, providing ample real estate for sticky campus maps, live transaction tickers, and dual-column item inspection screens.

## Elevation & Depth

This system adopts a hybrid "Soft Neo-Brutalist" elevation model: sharp structural borders paired with offset hard-edge shadows and occasional translucent glass planes.

- **Base Layer (Level 0):** Flat `#FAFAFD` ground.
- **Card Tier (Level 1):** Solid `#FFFFFF` background with a crisp `1.5px solid #12111A` outline and a flat offset shadow: `2px 2px 0px #12111A`.
- **Interactive Hover / Accent Tier (Level 2):** Elevated interactive components (cards on hover, active drop items) translate `-2px, -2px` and project a boosted offset shadow: `4px 4px 0px #12111A`.
- **Overlay & Sheet Tier (Level 3):** Modal overlays and sticky navigation bars introduce light glassmorphism (`background: rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85); backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`) framed by a sharp `1.5px solid #12111A` bottom or surrounding border, keeping modern depth tactile.

## Shapes

The design uses balanced, rounded geometry (level `2`) layered with full-pill elements to create an energetic contrast between functional cards and sticker-like badges.

- **Cards & Sheets:** Base radius of `0.5rem` (8px), scaling to `1rem` (16px) on primary marketplace feed tiles.
- **Badges, Pills, & Action Buttons:** Full pill geometry (`rounded-full` / 9999px) for search inputs, category pills, live status indicators, and main CTA buttons.
- **Sticker Accents:** Intentional subtle rotational offsets (`-1.5deg` to `+2deg`) on featured tags and promo labels to emulate physical sticker culture.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Pill-shaped, background `#FF334B`, text `#FFFFFF`, border `2px solid #12111A`, offset shadow `3px 3px 0px #12111A`. On press, translates `+2px, +2px` with shadow reduced to `1px 1px 0px #12111A`.
- **Secondary Action:** Pill-shaped, background `#7C3AED`, text `#FFFFFF`, border `2px solid #12111A`, offset shadow `3px 3px 0px #12111A`.
- **Ghost / Tertiary:** Transparent background, text `#12111A`, border `1.5px dashed #12111A`, shifting to solid on hover.

### Badges & Pill Chips
- **Campus Location Chips:** Pill format, light background tint with `1.5px solid #12111A`, housing an emoji or icon paired with bold uppercase text (e.g., `📍 TORRE A · PISO 3`).
- **Live Status ("Disponible ahora"):** Background `#00E599`, text `#12111A`, `1.5px solid #12111A`, featuring an active pulsing green dot indicator.
- **Category Filter Chips:** Neutral white pills with `1.5px solid #12111A`. Selected state inverts to `#12111A` background with white text or pops in `#7C3AED`.

### Cards
- **Marketplace Listing Card:** White surface, `1.5px solid #12111A`, corner radius `16px`, shadow `3px 3px 0px #12111A`. Top section contains an aspect-ratio-locked image with rounded-t corners, overlaid with a floating price tag pill (`#FF334B` text on white badge) and top-right seller avatar badge. Bottom section features title, timestamp, and location tag.

### Input Fields
- **Search & Filter Bars:** Pill shape with `2px solid #12111A` border, background `#FFFFFF`, shadow `2px 2px 0px #12111A`. Focus state adds an electric outline glow in `#FF334B` without losing the dark border.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- **Selection Controls:** Custom chunky square (`rounded-sm`) or circle controls with `2px solid #12111A`. Checked state fills with `#00E599` or `#FF334B` and displays a bold black checkmark.
